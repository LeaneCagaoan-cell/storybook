Horimiya

by Hero & Daisuke Hagiwara

Romance • Comedy • Slice of Life

The Two Faces

Kyoko Hori is bright and popular at school, but quiet and plain at home. Izumi Miyamura seems gloomy and quiet in class, but outside he has tattoos, piercings, and a gentle heart. When they discover each other's secrets, they promise to keep them — and a beautiful friendship begins.

As they spend more time together, they learn that people are never just one thing. Hori sees the kindness beneath Miyamura's quiet exterior, and Miyamura sees the warmth beneath Hori's bright smile. Slowly, their friendship blossoms into something deeper — a love that accepts every side of who they are.

Their story is just beginning... 💜
